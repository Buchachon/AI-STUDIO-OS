# MUSIC_BRIEF

## Goal

Create a 2-hour deep focus music experience for work, study, and long concentration sessions.

## Mood

- Serious
- Cinematic
- Minimal
- Productive
- Premium

## Sonic Direction

Use a restrained deep focus sound with steady pacing, clean low-end, subtle ambient texture, and no distracting melodic hooks. The music should feel like a high-end studio control room: focused, precise, and quietly intense.

## Arrangement Notes

- Keep energy stable for long work sessions.
- Avoid sudden drops, loud transitions, or vocals.
- Favor smooth evolution over dramatic changes.
- Maintain a professional, modern, cinematic atmosphere.

## Duration Target

2 hours
